Wonders of Vietnam - Interactive Photo Gallery

An accessible, interactive gallery exploring Vietnam's breathtaking natural landscapes and cultural heritage.

🖱️ Mouse Interaction: Hover over any thumbnail to preview in the main frame. Move away to reset.
⌨️ Keyboard Interaction: Press Tab / Shift + Tab to focus through images. The preview updates on focus and resets on blur.
